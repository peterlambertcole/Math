Title: Minimal symplectic 4-manifolds are smoothly irreducible

Abstract: We show that every smoothly embedded 3-sphere in a symplectic 4-manifold bounds a blowup of the standard 4-ball on one side.  To do this, we use a symplectic 1-cocycle on a relative trisection to construct a weak symplectic filling with $S^3$-boundary.  This implies that every homotopy 4-ball that embeds into a symplectic 4-manifold is standard.  Consequently, we establish the smooth 4-dimensional Schoenflies conjecture and prove that many potential counterexamples to the smooth 4-dimensional Poincare conjecture, such as Gluck twists and Cappell-Shaneson homotopy spheres, are standard.
